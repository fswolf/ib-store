Mail

Order and password reset emails go out through the server's mail as (MAIL_FROM in config/config.php). Outgoing mail is off: MAIL_FROM in config/config.php is empty or php mail() is missing on this server.

Payments

Bitcoin through BTCPay Server at is on. Webhook: /api/payment/btcpay/webhook Bitcoin is off. Fill in the BTCPAY_* settings in config/config.php to turn it on (docs/README.md has the steps).
Cards through Stripe Checkout are on. Webhook: /api/payment/stripe/webhook Cards are off. Fill in STRIPE_SECRET_KEY and STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET in config/config.php to turn them on.
PayPal is on. Webhook (optional): /api/payment/paypal/webhook PayPal is off. Fill in PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID and PAYPAL_SECRET in config/config.php to turn it on.
